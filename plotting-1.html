07 — FAQs

Everything You Need to Know

Clear, transparent answers regarding title certifications, development sanctions, loan eligibility, and custom bungalow construction.

Yes. Green Valley Township is fully sanctioned by the Pune Metropolitan Region Development Authority (PMRDA Layout Order: PMRDA/TPS/2024/098) and registered under MahaRERA (Registration No: P52100054321). All title clearances, layout approvals, and escrow account details are officially documented and available for solicitor verification.

Yes. Green Valley Township is pre-approved by leading financial institutions including State Bank of India (SBI), HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and Axis Bank. Eligible buyers can secure up to 75% to 80% funding on the plot value as well as composite plot-plus-construction loans at attractive home loan interest rates.

Every plot enjoys sanctioned permission for G+2 residential villa construction with an independent terrace. You have full architectural freedom to build your bespoke bungalow within PMRDA development control rules, or you may utilize our pre-approved architect floor plans for accelerated development.

Possession and registry are immediate. The township infrastructure—including asphalt blacktop roads, boundary demarcation stones, water supply tapping valves, and underground power conduits—is already delivered on-ground. Registry is executed at the sub-registrar office within 15–30 days of statutory agreement.

The township provides 40 and 60 ft. wide asphalt roads, concealed storm drainage, dedicated underground electrical conduits, central water supply, 24/7 security with CCTV surveillance, and landscaped botanical gardens. A professional facilities management agency oversees road sweeping, street lighting, and green maintenance.

Absolutely. Under RBI general permissions, NRIs and OCIs can freely invest in residential plots in India using standard NRE/NRO banking channels. Our specialized One Roof NRI Advisory team provides seamless remote video verification, Power of Attorney (POA) documentation, and repatriation assistance.
