02 — Project Overview

A Master-Planned 80-Acre Township Engineered for Generational Value

Green Valley Township is Pune's landmark plotting development situated along the high-appreciation North Expressway corridor. Conceptualized as an eco-sustainable gated community, the project harmoniously marries urban infrastructure with serene natural surroundings.

Unlike raw unapproved agricultural subdivisions, every single plot in Green Valley is PMRDA sanctioned, RERA registered, and has an independent Non-Agricultural (NA 44) Collector Sanction with demarcated brass-pin survey markers.

Institutional Precision & Freehold Security
