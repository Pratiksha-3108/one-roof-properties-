Privacy & Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 27, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

At One Roof Properties LLC, we are committed to upholding strict privacy standards for all clients, property investors, homeowners, and overseas NRI clients. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, safeguard, and disclose your personal and financial information when you access our portal or engage our real estate brokerage services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Personal Identification: Full legal name, date of birth, passport/national identification details, email address, phone number, and physical mailing address.
  • Financial & Transaction Data: Loan pre-approval letters, banking documentation for proof of funds, source of funds disclosures, and NRE/NRO account verification for international transactions.
  • Property Search Criteria: Desired locations, plot dimensions, bedroom preferences, budget limits, and saved favorite listings.

3. Purpose of Processing

We collect data solely to facilitate legitimate real estate transactions, including: scheduling site inspections, conducting 30-year title deed searches, submitting purchase offers, coordinating escrow deposits, preparing Power of Attorney documentation, and complying with RERA / anti-money laundering (AML) laws.

4. Security & Data Protection

We utilize enterprise-grade 256-bit SSL encryption for data in transit and encrypted storage vaults for all transaction files. Access to sensitive title documents and KYC dossiers is restricted exclusively to authorized escrow officers and certified brokers handling your transaction.

5. Third-Party Disclosures

We never sell, lease, or monetize your personal data. Disclosures are made strictly to sanctioned closing partners: government registry offices, partner mortgage institutions, certified property valuers, and vetted legal title attorneys.
