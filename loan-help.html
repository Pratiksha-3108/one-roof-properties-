FINANCIAL ADVISORY

Expert Guidance for NRI Land & Home Loans

Acquiring a land or property loan in India as a Non-Resident Indian can involve specific regulatory frameworks. One Roof Properties simplifies your journey through end-to-end institutional support and banking partnerships.

QUALIFICATIONS

Who Qualifies for NRI Housing & Plot Loans?

  • Indian citizens residing abroad for employment, profession, or business purposes.
  • Government personnel and diplomats posted abroad with Indian missions.
  • Officials deputed abroad with multilateral organizations (e.g. World Bank, IMF, UN agencies).
  • State Government or PSU officials on overseas assignments holding valid Indian passports.
Loans are readily available for purchasing sanctioned NA plots, constructing luxury villas, plot enhancements, and property acquisition across One Roof Properties estates.
LOAN TERMS

Eligibility Criteria & Overview

Interest rates for NRIs are aligned with premier Indian banking benchmarks. Lending institutions assess eligibility based on monthly income stability, work visa validity, credit history (including overseas credit bureaus where applicable), assets, and repayment track record.

REGULATIONS

RBI Directives & Funding Limits

MAXIMUM FUNDING
Loan amounts generally cover up to 80%-85% of property cost, with the remaining margin paid through authorized banking channels.
REPAYMENT NORMS
EMIs must be routed through NRE or NRO bank accounts in accordance with FEMA guidelines.
LOAN RANGE
Flexible sanction limits tailored to individual profile, investment size, and repayment tenure.

Documentation Required

Documentation requirements are structured to ensure full compliance with RBI regulations:

PERSONAL & EMPLOYMENT ID
  • Valid Passport & Visa / Work Permit
  • Employment Contract / Appointment Letter
  • Last 3–6 Months Salary Slips
  • Overseas & NRE/NRO Bank Statements
PROPERTY & LEGAL RECORDS
  • Clear Title Verification Report
  • Sanctioned NA Order & Layout Plan
  • Nil-Encumbrance Certificate (EC)
  • Registered Agreement for Sale
