STATUTORY DEFINITION

NRI Recognition under FERA & FEMA

Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs/OCIs) are recognized under the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA) and Reserve Bank of India (RBI) guidelines. Every premier banking institution and housing finance company defines an NRI as:

“An Indian citizen who holds a valid Indian passport and resides outside India for employment, business, or vocation under circumstances indicating an intention for an indefinite duration of stay abroad.”
LEGAL PROTOCOLS

Core Legal & Procedural Framework

Essential regulatory guidelines applicable to NRI land and residential property investments in India:

• RBI GENERAL PERMISSIONS
NRIs holding valid Indian passports and OCIs do not require any prior RBI approvals for purchasing sanctioned residential plots or homes.
• FEMA COMPLIANT BANKING
All investment payments must be routed through standard banking channels via inward remittance or NRE, NRO, or FCNR accounts.
• TITLE DUE DILIGENCE
One Roof Properties provides comprehensive 30-year Title Search Reports from reputed solicitors certifying clear, marketable ownership.
• CLEARANCE & ENVIRONMENTAL APPROVALS
All our estates have certified sanctions including Town Planning (PMRDA/Collectorate), non-agricultural permissions, and utility zoning.
FINANCIAL SECURITY

Repatriation of Funds & Power of Attorney

• REPATRIATION OF SALE PROCEEDS
Sale proceeds of up to two residential properties can be fully repatriated overseas through authorized dealer bank channels, subject to applicable tax compliance (Form 15CA/15CB).
• SPECIAL POWER OF ATTORNEY (POA)
If you cannot travel to India for registration, you can execute a Special Power of Attorney in favor of a trusted family member or legal representative, notarized and adjudicated at the Indian Consulate.
