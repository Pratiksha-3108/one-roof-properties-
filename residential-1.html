Frequently Asked Questions

What configurations are available?
Skyview Residency offers meticulously crafted 2 BHK (750 - 850 sq.ft.) and 3 BHK (1,050 - 1,250 sq.ft.) luxury apartments with private viewing decks.
What is the starting price?
Pricing begins at ₹ 1.25 Cr* onwards for 2 BHK configurations and ₹ 1.75 Cr* onwards for 3 BHK layouts, exclusive of government taxes and registration.
Is the project ready to move?
Skyview Residency is currently under active construction with MahaRERA-approved milestones. Handover and possession are scheduled for December 2026.
What amenities are available?
Residents enjoy access to a resort swimming pool, high-tech gymnasium, clubhouse, children's play park, landscaped green spaces, 24/7 biometric security, and covered parking.
Where is the project located?
The project is located in central Kothrud, Pune, just 3 minutes from D'Mart, 10 minutes from the nearest Pune Metro station, and 15 minutes from Pune University.
Can I schedule a site visit?
Yes, complimentary VIP site visits with personalized property consultation can be scheduled online via our enquiry form or via direct WhatsApp concierge.
