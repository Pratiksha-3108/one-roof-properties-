What documents are required to make a purchase offer?
To submit a binding residential purchase offer, you will typically need a Government-issued Photo ID, an official Mortgage Pre-Approval Letter from a recognized lender (or verified Proof of Funds bank statement if paying cash), and an Earnest Money Deposit (usually 1% to 3% of the total purchase price).
What is the difference between Pre-Qualification and Pre-Approval?
Pre-qualification is an informal estimate based on self-reported financial data. Pre-approval, however, involves rigorous underwriting review of your tax filings, paystubs, W-2s, and credit score. A Pre-Approval letter makes your purchase offer substantially more attractive to luxury property sellers.
How does One Roof determine the market listing price?
Our pricing advisors conduct an in-depth Comparative Market Analysis (CMA), examining recent closed transactions within 0.5 miles, active competition, architectural upgrades, seasonal buyer demand, and square footage utility to arrive at an optimal valuation strategy.
What is an Escrow account and how does it protect my funds?
An escrow account is an independent neutral third-party holding facility where earnest money deposits and closing funds are held securely until all inspection contingencies, title clearances, and lender instructions are completely fulfilled.
